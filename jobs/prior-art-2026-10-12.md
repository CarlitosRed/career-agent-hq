## Takeaways
1. Borrow, do not rebuild: career-ops for scanning, scoring, ATS checks (already extracted into jobs/scoring-rubric.md); Resume Matcher or amlei-resume ideas for "one source, many outputs".
2. Our real niche, nobody found: a private skills lake with fact levels and proof, plus a side-hustle scout, plus a gap-to-project builder tied to PO Reader and this project, with the portfolio as output.
3. Warning from the sources: gap tools invent gaps unless given both the real posting and the real profile. Our lake plus posting text solves that.
4. Cheap-run pattern seen: local scan scripts + small model scoring + stronger model only for drafts.
